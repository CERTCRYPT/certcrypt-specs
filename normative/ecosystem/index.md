## Purpose

This page indexes the active CERTCRYPT ecosystem categories.

The ecosystem layer defines the artifacts and contracts that bind issuance, capacity authorization, settlement and verification, and the operator specifications that relate nodes to them, across the active public corpus, without redefining the foundational interpretation boundary or the protocol envelopes that they consume.
