## Purpose

This page indexes the active verification-layer artifacts of the CERTCRYPT ecosystem.

Verification specifications define the offline-portable artifact that carries everything needed to verify a CERTCRYPT certificate (`verification-package/1`) and the minimum fail-closed verifier behavior under public rules (`verifier-toolkit/1`).

## Boundary

Verification specifications define artifact composition and minimum verifier behavior; they do not by themselves create authority, replace the rules domain, or produce `Independent` outside what the carried artifacts already establish under public rules.

## One-line summary

The verification category defines the artifact and verifier-behavior specifications that make CERTCRYPT certificate evaluation reproducible offline under public rules.
