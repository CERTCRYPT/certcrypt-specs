## Boundary

CCCP defines canonicalization, hashing, and commitment construction only. It does not define certificate envelope serialization, declared-context validation, settlement, or verifier policy by itself.

## One-line summary

CCCP is the protocol family for deterministic, zero-data canonicalization, hashing, and commitment construction in CERTCRYPT.
