This page indexes the active CERTCRYPT protocol families.

Protocols define canonicalization, certificate-envelope, commitment-scheme, consistency-verification, and settlement-path rules within the active public normative corpus.

## Boundary

Protocols define formal processing and verification rules.
They do not by themselves prove truth, create institutional authority, or collapse proof into validation.
