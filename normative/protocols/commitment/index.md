## Purpose

This page indexes the active CERTCRYPT commitment protocol families.

Commitment protocols define interoperable commitment-scheme, root-construction, and proof-verification rules that may be referenced by higher-layer subject and verifier workflows.

## Boundary

Commitment protocols define commitment and proof mechanics only.
They do not by themselves prove truth, create institutional authority, or collapse proof into validation.

## One-line summary

The CERTCRYPT commitment protocol layer defines interoperable commitment-scheme and proof-verification rules under active public versions.
