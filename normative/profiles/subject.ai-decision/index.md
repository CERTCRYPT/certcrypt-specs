## Boundary

`subject.ai-decision` is a subject-canonicalization family for one observable model invocation and decision per record. It does not define issuance, Proof of Independence, model quality, provider identity, execution causality, or the correctness of any decision by itself.

## One-line summary

**subject.ai-decision is the subject-canonicalization family for binding one observable model invocation, its ordered material descriptors, and its decision as deterministic canonical bytes, never the decision's correctness.**
