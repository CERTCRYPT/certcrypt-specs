## Boundary

`independence.proof` defines one CERTCRYPT model family. It does not by itself replace issuance, settlement, cryptographic verification, or Proof of Independence evaluation.

Within that family, Proof of Independence is the structural result under public rules, while `independence_proof` is the canonical artifact family used to represent that result.

## One-line summary

**independence.proof is the model family for offline-verifiable artifacts representing that a specific leaf reached `Independent` under public rules.**
